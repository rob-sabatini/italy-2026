Monday, May 25, 2026

Polignano half-day (Lama Monachile, old town), walk-in lunch, pool afternoon at Borgobianco, dinner at Osteria Sangiovanni in Monopoli + harbor walk.

Vico Verzella 2. Reserved. Wheelchair accommodated.

SIDE QUESTS

⚡ 📍 Descend to Cala Porto cove (Rob & Maddison only)

Duration: 30 min  |  Logistics: Parents at a café in Piazza Aldo Moro (you arrived together)

Stairs down to the lower cove below Lama Monachile. Fewer crowds, real swim spot if weather's right. Parents stay at the same café in the piazza. Return to that spot.

⚡ 📍 Monopoli harbor walls at sunset (parents can join)

Duration: 30-40 min  |  Logistics: Walking together from harbor parking

Park at Monopoli harbor and walk the walls along the Adriatic. Flat sections are accessible — accessible chair can do most of it. Castello Carlo V is the centerpiece. Then dinner at Sangiovanni.

NOTES

  • Slow start — leave Borgobianco ~10:30 AM after breakfast.
  • Polignano lunch: pick whichever fits the mood. All walk-in.
  • Skip Grotta Palazzese — stairs only, dinner only, expensive.
  • Cala Porto cove descent is stairs-only — Maddison/Rob side-quest while parents wait.
  • Pool/spa at Borgobianco in afternoon before Monopoli dinner.
  • Monopoli ~10 min drive. Park at the harbor for sunset walk.
