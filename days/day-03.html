Saturday, May 23, 2026

Open day in Rome — explore at your pace. Meet cousin at Cimitero Flaminio at 4:30/5 PM for the visit, then go directly to family dinner at Casette di Campagna together.

💡 ROME DAY OPTIONS — pick what fits the energy

📍 Borghese Gardens + Pincio Terrace

Vast park with paved paths, perfect for the chair. Maddison can rent a rowboat on the lake (€3/20min). Pincio Terrace overlook is the postcard Rome view. ~10 min walk from Aleph.

📍 Galleria Doria Pamphilj

Private aristocratic palace with Velázquez's Pope Innocent X portrait + Caravaggio + Raphael. Audio guide narrated by the current prince. Quiet, accessible. Rome's best-kept museum secret.

📍 Centrale Montemartini — sculpture in a power plant

Roman marble statues inside a converted early-20th-century power plant. Surreal contrast of classical against industrial. Far less crowded than Capitoline. Wheelchair-accessible. €11. ~20 min taxi.

📍 Aventine Keyhole + Giardino degli Aranci

Look through the famous keyhole — frames St. Peter's dome perfectly through cypress trees. Next door, Orange Garden has one of Rome's best free panoramas. Wheelchair-friendly. ~15 min taxi.

📍 Quartiere Coppedè — hidden Art Nouveau

Tiny fairytale-architecture neighborhood most tourists miss. Stained glass, chimera fountains, painted facades. ~20 min taxi. Flat, quick visit, real local discovery.

📍 Long lunch + nap at Aleph

Honestly fine. After 3 days of movement, a long lunch nearby and a real nap before the cemetery is a legitimate plan.
