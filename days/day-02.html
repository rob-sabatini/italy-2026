Day 2 — Imperial Rome Golf Cart Tour + Trastevere

Friday, May 22, 2026

3-hour private golf cart tour of Imperial Rome (10:30 AM pickup at Aleph). Tour is customizable — work with the driver on the day to hit the spots that matter most. Dinner at Checco er Carrettiere in Trastevere.

💡 TOUR HIGHLIGHTS — what to ask the driver to include

Mom wants to see the Trevi Fountain — make sure this is on the route. Other must-hits worth requesting: Colosseum exterior (the iconic view), Roman Forum / Palatine Hill overlook, Piazza Venezia + the Vittoriano, Circus Maximus, Pantheon (cobblestone but doable in the cart), and Piazza Navona. The driver will tailor based on your interests and pace — just speak up early. Three hours covers a lot if you skip getting out at every stop.

SIDE QUESTS

⚡ 📍 Quartiere Coppedè — hidden Art Nouveau neighborhood (Rob & Maddison only)

Duration: 60 min  |  Logistics: Parents resting at Aleph — taxi ~15 min each way

Tiny fairytale-architecture quarter most tourists never find. Stained glass, chimera fountains, frog statues, painted facades. Real local discovery during the afternoon rest. Parents stay at Aleph.

⚡ 📍 Antico Caffè Greco (Rob & Maddison only — or parents can join later)

Duration: 45 min  |  Logistics: 10 min walk from Aleph

Rome's oldest café (1760). Goethe, Keats, Casanova all sat here. Red velvet, marble, oil paintings. Worth a cappuccino at the counter just to be in the room. Parents stay at Aleph during rest — or join if they want a coffee out.
